# 🚩 05: Agile Anti-Patterns - Warning Signs

**Goal:** To help junior developers recognize "Agile in Name Only" (AINO) behaviors. Recognizing these anti-patterns early allows the team to address them during Retrospectives and return to true Agile values.

## 📉 Common Agile Anti-Patterns

### 1. "Water-Scrum-Fall"
**The Symptom:** The team uses Sprints and Daily Standups, but there is still a massive "Requirements Phase" at the beginning and a massive "Testing/Hardening Phase" at the end.
**The Reality:** This is just Waterfall with a different vocabulary. It fails because it delays the most critical feedback loops until the end of the project.
**The Fix:** Focus on **Vertical Slices** and **Incremental Delivery**. Every Sprint should result in a potentially shippable increment.

### 2. "The Fragile Agile" (Rigid Adherence)
**The Symptom:** The team follows the "rules" of a framework (like Scrum) perfectly, but ignores the actual values. For example, refusing to change a Sprint goal because "the rules say we can't," even when the goal is clearly no longer valuable.
**The Reality:** This is prioritizing *Process* over *Individuals and Interactions*. 
**The Fix:** Remember that frameworks are tools, not laws. When a process rule hinders value delivery, use the Retrospective to adapt the process.

### 3. "The Cargo Cult"
**The Symptom:** Performing the rituals (Standups, Grooming, Retros) without understanding *why* they are done. The meetings become status reports rather than synchronization points.
**The Reality:** The team is mimicking the form of Agile without adopting the mindset.
**The Fix:** Re-read the **Agile Manifesto**. Ask "What value does this meeting provide?" If the answer is "because we've always done it," it's time to redefine the purpose of the event.

### 4. "The Proxy Product Owner"
**The Symptom:** The PO doesn't have the authority to make decisions and must "check with the real stakeholders" for every single item.
**The Reality:** This creates a massive bottleneck and destroys the "Customer Collaboration" principle.
**The Fix:** Empower the PO or ensure the team has direct, periodic access to the end-users for feedback.
