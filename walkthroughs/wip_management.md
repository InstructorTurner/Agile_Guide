# 🌊 WIP Walkthrough: Stop Starting, Start Finishing

Your mission today is to shift your focus from "being busy" (occupancy) to "getting things done" (throughput). The goal is to minimize context switching and maximize the flow of value.

## 🎯 Final Deliverable
By the end of this practice, you should achieve:

1. A personal **WIP Limit of 1**: Only one active item in progress at a time.
2. A successful "Swarm": Helping a teammate clear a ticket before starting your own new work.

---

## 🛠️ The Workflow

### Phase 1: The "One Thing" Rule (Focus)
Context switching is a productivity killer. Every time you jump between tasks, you lose mental momentum.

- **Activity:** Audit your current board. Prefer only having 1 item in progress at a time whenever possible.
- **The Action:** Identify the highest priority item among your active tasks. Focus entirely on that.
- **The Rule:** Resist the urge to start a new ticket just because you are waiting for a PR review or a response to a question.

### Phase 2: The "Stuck" Protocol (Collaborate)
"Ghost WIP" happens when a ticket stays in progress but isn't actually moving. Avoid this by following these steps when you hit a wall:

1. **The Reach Out:** Do not struggle in silence. Ping the team or a lead for a quick sync or a pairing session.
2. **The Swarm:** Instead of picking up a new ticket, ask: *"Can anyone help me push this item to Done?"* or *"Who can I help to get their ticket across the finish line?"*
3. **The Block:** If collaboration doesn't resolve the issue, move the item to "Blocked." Add a clear note explaining exactly what is needed to unblock it.

### Phase 3: The Pull Decision (Priority)
When your WIP is 0, don't just grab the first thing you see. Use this logic to decide what to pull next:

1. **Check the Finish Line (Swarm first):** Look at the columns to the right (Review, Testing). Can you help a teammate finish something? Clearing an existing item is always higher priority than starting a new one.
2. **Check the Priority:** If no one needs help, pull the highest priority item from the top of the backlog.
3. **The Gut Check:** Ask yourself: *"Am I pulling this because it's the most important thing for the team, or because I'm avoiding a harder task?"*

---

## ✅ Flow Health Checklist
Before you move a card to "In Progress," run this audit:

- [ ] If I am blocked, have I attempted to collaborate/swarm before moving the ticket?
- [ ] Have I checked if I can help "pull" an existing item to "Done" first?
- [ ] Is this the highest priority item available?
