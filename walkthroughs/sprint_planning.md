# 🚀 Sprint Planning Walkthrough

Your mission today is to move from a "rough wishlist" to a committed, actionable Sprint Backlog for Sprint 2.

## 🎯 Final Deliverable
A fully populated Scrum Board containing:

1. A clearly defined **Sprint Goal**.
2. A **Sprint Backlog** consisting of User Stories and their corresponding implementation tasks.
3. A **Product Backlog** that has been updated with new and refined ideas.

---

## 🛠️ The Workflow

### Phase 1: The Brain Dump (Capture)
Before you plan the sprint, ensure your Product Backlog is complete.

- **Activity:** As a team, spend 30 minutes adding every "I think we need..." or "What about...?" idea as a card in the Product Backlog.
- **Rule:** No idea is too small or too early. If it needs to be done eventually, it goes on the board.

### Phase 2: The Slicing Session (Refine)
Turn vague ideas into "Ready" work.

1. **Prioritize:** Your designated Product Owner (PO) should move the most valuable items to the top of the Product Backlog.
2. **Slice:** Take the top items and ensure they are **Vertical Slices**. If a story is too big to finish in one sprint, split it.
3. **Define:** Every story must follow the format: *"As a [User], I want to [Goal], so that [Benefit]."*
4. **Verify:** Add **Acceptance Criteria (AC)** to every story. If you can't test it, it's not ready.


### Phase 3: Defining the Mission (Plan)
Build your commitment for Sprint 2.

1. **Set the Goal:** Agree on one concise **Sprint Goal**. (e.g., *"Enable users to edit and delete their events"*).
2. **Select Stories:** Pull stories from the top of the Product Backlog that directly support this goal.
3. **Task Out:** For each selected story, break it down into technical implementation tasks. Ensure these tasks also represent vertical progress (DB -> API -> UI).

---

## ✅ Definition of Ready (Checklist)
Before you finish, audit your board. A story is only "Ready" if:

- [ ] It follows the User Story format.
- [ ] It has clear, testable Acceptance Criteria.
- [ ] It is a "Vertical Slice" (delivers a working piece of functionality).
- [ ] It is small enough to be completed within the sprint.
- [ ] It aligns with the Sprint Goal.
