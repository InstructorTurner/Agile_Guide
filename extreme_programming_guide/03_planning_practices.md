# Planning Practices

XP planning is focused on flexibility and close collaboration with the customer.

## User Stories (XP Style)
**What it is**: Short, simple descriptions of a feature told from the perspective of the user. In XP, these are often treated as "promises for a conversation."
**Best Practice**: Keep stories small enough to be completed in a few days.
**Scrum Integration**: XP stories fit perfectly into the Scrum Product Backlog; focus on the "conversation" during Sprint Planning.

## The Planning Game
**What it is**: A collaborative process where the customer defines the value (what) and the developers define the cost (how long).
**Best Practice**: Use relative estimation (e.g., story points) rather than absolute hours.
**Scrum Integration**: This is the essence of Sprint Planning and Product Backlog Refinement.

## Small Releases (Planning Perspective)
**What it is**: Planning the product in a series of small, iterative versions.
**Best Practice**: Prioritize the most valuable and highest-risk features first.
**Scrum Integration**: Helps the Product Owner manage the Product Goal by breaking it into achievable Sprint Goals.
