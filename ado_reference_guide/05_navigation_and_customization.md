# 🛠️ Navigation and Customization

Azure DevOps provides several ways to filter noise and customize your view so you can focus on your specific tasks without getting lost in the project's total volume of work.

## 🏷️ Using Tags and Labels

Tags are a flexible way to categorize work items without changing their formal hierarchy. They are visible as small colored pills on the cards in the Sprint Board and Backlog.

### How to add tags:
1. Open a work item.
2. Locate the **Tags** field (usually near the top right or under the title).
3. Type your tag and press Enter.

**Commonly used tags**

* `Blocked`: Indicates you cannot proceed until something else happens.
* `Needs-Review`: The work is done, but you are waiting for a peer's eyes.
* `Tech-Debt`: Marks work that is a temporary fix and needs a permanent solution later.

## 🔍 Advanced Filtering

When the backlog has hundreds of items, you need to filter. Navigate to **Boards > Backlogs** or **Boards > Sprints** and use the filter icon (funnel) in the top right.

**Useful filter combinations:**
* **Assigned To**: Set this to "Me" to see only your current responsibilities.
* **State**: Filter by "To Do" to see what's next in the queue.
* **Tags**: Filter by `Blocked` to see everything currently stopping progress.

## 📝 Retrospectives

We use the built-in Retrospectives tool to improve our process. Navigate to **Boards > Retrospectives**.

**How it works:**
1. **Create a Card**: Click "Add card" to share a thought on what went well or what could be improved.
2. **Voting**: During the meeting, use the "Vote" button on cards to highlight the most important topics for discussion.
3. **Action Items**: The Scrum Master will convert the top-voted cards into "Action Items," which are then tracked as work items in the backlog.

## 📊 Customizing Your View

In the **Boards > Backlogs** view, you can change which columns are visible.

1. Click the **Column Options** (gear icon) in the top right.
2. Check or uncheck fields like **Story Points**, **Remaining Work**, or **Priority**.
3. This allows you to see at a glance how much work is left without opening every single ticket.
