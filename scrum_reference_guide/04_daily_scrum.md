# 🧍‍♂️ 04: Daily Scrum (Standup) - Synchronization and Impediments

**Goal:** A fast, time-boxed daily meeting (15 minutes absolute maximum) to synchronize the team, review progress toward the Sprint Goal, and identify roadblocks.

## ⏲️ The Rules of Engagement
1.  **Timebox:** **15 Minutes.** If it takes longer, break it into follow-up discussion groups.
2.  **Standing:** Stand up! It helps keep the meeting brief and focused.
3.  **Focus:** The discussion must revolve around the *Sprint Goal*. Is the team on track?

## 🤝 The Format (Per Person)
Each team member should briefly answer:

1.  **What did I accomplish yesterday?** (What progress was made on the committed stories?)
2.  **What will I accomplish today?** (What is the plan for the next 24 hours?)
3.  **Are there any Impediments?** (Anything blocking my progress? A dependency? A required piece of information?)

### 🛑 The Anti-Pattern Alert: Problem Solving!
**NEVER** solve a technical problem or spend time negotiating solutions in the Daily Scrum.
*   **What to do:** When someone raises an impediment or a dependency, the team acknowledges it, and then *immediately* schedules a follow-up meeting with only the necessary people afterward.
*   **Goal:** The Daily Scrum is for *reporting status* and *identifying risk*, not for *solving risk*.
