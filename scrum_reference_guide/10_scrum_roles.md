# 👥 10: Scrum Roles & Responsibilities

**Goal:** To clearly define who does what within the Scrum framework to ensure accountability and avoid overlap.

## 1. The Product Owner (The "What")
**Description:** The single point of accountability for maximizing the value of the product. They act as the bridge between stakeholders and the team.

**Common Tasks:**

*   Defining the Product Goal and ordering the backlog to optimize value.
*   **Collaborating with Developers** to refine stories and ensure they are "Ready" for a sprint.
*   Ensuring the team understands the "Why" and the value for the end-user.
*   Working *with* the team to balance business needs with technical sustainability.

## 2. The Scrum Master (The "How")
**Description:** A true leader who serves the Scrum Team and the larger organization by removing impediments and coaching the team on Scrum theory.

**Common Tasks:**

*   Facilitating Scrum events (Planning, Daily Scrum, Review, Retro).
*   Removing "blockers" that prevent the team from making progress.
*   Coaching the team in self-management and cross-functionality.
*   Protecting the team from outside interruptions during the Sprint.

## 3. The Developers (The "Execution")
**Description:** The people committed to creating any aspect of a usable Increment each Sprint. They are cross-functional and self-managing.

**Common Tasks:**

*   Creating the plan for the Sprint (the Sprint Backlog).
*   Instilling quality by adhering to the Definition of Done.
*   Adapting their plan daily toward the Sprint Goal.
*   Collaborating (Pairing/Swarming) to finish work.

## 4. Stakeholders (The "Influence")
**Description:** External individuals or groups with a vested interest in the product (customers, executives, users).

**Common Tasks:**

*   Providing feedback during the Sprint Review.
*   Sharing business requirements and market needs with the Product Owner.
*   Testing the Increment to ensure it meets their needs.
