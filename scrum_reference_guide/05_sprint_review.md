# 📊 05: Sprint Review - Showing the Value

**Goal:** To demonstrate to stakeholders and the Product Owner what we have *completed* during the Sprint, gather feedback, and adjust the Product Backlog accordingly.

## 🤝 Participants
*   **Core Team:** Essential.
*   **Product Owner:** Leads the discussion and gathers feedback.
*   **Stakeholders:** Anyone who uses, sponsors, or relies on the product.

## ✅ The Process: Proof, Don't Discussion
This meeting is a live, working demonstration.

1.  **What happened:** The team presents the fully working, accepted work (the vertical slices) that was completed.
2.  **What is the feedback:** Stakeholders ask questions about the functionality and how they could improve it.
3.  **What's next:** The Product Owner updates the Product Backlog based on the collective feedback, potentially creating new, high-priority stories for the next Sprint.

### 🧠 Key Reminder: The Review is NOT a Planning Session
*   **WRONG:** Using the Review to discuss what *might* happen next or what *could* be built.
*   **RIGHT:** Showing exactly what *was* built, giving stakeholders solid evidence to make decisions with.
